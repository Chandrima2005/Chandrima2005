## 👋 Hi, I'm Chandrima

I'm doing my **Master's in Robotics & AI at IIT Guwahati**, and I work as a **Junior Data Scientist at Calsoft**. I come from Electronics & Communication, so most of my work starts with a signal: acoustic pulses from a dark-matter detector, a pair of stereo images, or the pages of a PDF.

My thesis at the **Saha Institute of Nuclear Physics** used machine learning to tell neutrons from gamma rays — reaching up to 100% accuracy — and it's now being prepared for publication.
